# POL-02 — Acceptable Use Policy

| **Applies to** | All CivicStack employees, contractors and interns, and every device used for CivicStack work |
| **Implements** | NIST SP 800-53 Rev 5 PL-4, PL-4(1), AC-8, AC-20, MP-7, SI-3 · ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.10, A.6.2, A.6.7, A.7.7, A.8.1 |

**0.1** **Acknowledgment.** Everyone signs this policy (the Rules of Behavior) before getting access, and again each year. — *Satisfies: PL-4(c), PL-4(d) · ISO A.6.2*

---

## 1. Customer data

**1.1** Customer data, including applicant PII in HAP, may be accessed only to perform an assigned task. Looking up records out of curiosity, including your own, relatives' or public figures', is prohibited. — *Satisfies: PL-4(a), AC-6 · ISO A.5.10*

**1.2** Customer data may not be copied to personal devices, personal email, personal cloud storage or AI tools that are not on the approved-tools list. — *Satisfies: PL-4(a), AC-20, SC-7(10) · ISO A.5.10, A.8.12*

**1.3** Full SSNs may not be displayed, exported or pasted into tickets, chat or documents. Use the last four digits or the token. — *Satisfies: PL-4(a), PT-2 · ISO A.5.34, A.8.11*

## 2. Devices

**2.1** Only CivicStack-managed devices may reach production or customer data. Managed devices must keep disk encryption, EDR, automatic updates and the screen lock enabled. — *Satisfies: AC-19, AC-20 · ISO A.8.1*

**2.2** Do not disable, bypass or uninstall security tools. — *Satisfies: PL-4(a), SI-3 · ISO A.8.7*

**2.3** Removable media may not be used with customer data. — *Satisfies: MP-7 · ISO A.7.10*

**2.4** Lock your screen when you step away. No customer data is left visible or printed in shared spaces. — *Satisfies: AC-11 · ISO A.7.7*

## 3. Accounts and credentials

**3.1** Never share passwords, MFA devices or sessions. Use the company password manager. — *Satisfies: IA-5, PL-4(a) · ISO A.5.17*

**3.2** Never use a CivicStack password for any other service. — *Satisfies: IA-5 · ISO A.5.17*

## 4. Remote and public work

**4.1** On public networks, connect only through the company-managed secure access client. Do not work on customer data where others can see your screen. — *Satisfies: AC-17, PL-4(a) · ISO A.6.7*

## 5. Communications and social media

**5.1** Do not discuss customers, incidents or security weaknesses on social media or in public forums. — *Satisfies: PL-4(1) · ISO A.6.6*

## 6. Reporting

**6.1** Report suspected incidents, phishing, lost devices or policy violations straight away to `#security` or `security@`. Reporting in good faith is never punished. — *Satisfies: IR-6, PL-4(a) · ISO A.6.8*

## 7. Monitoring

**7.1** CivicStack systems are monitored. Users have no expectation of privacy on company systems, except where law provides otherwise. — *Satisfies: AC-8, AU-2 · ISO A.8.16*

## 8. Enforcement

**8.1** Violations may result in removal of access and disciplinary action up to termination. — *Satisfies: PS-8 · ISO A.6.4*
